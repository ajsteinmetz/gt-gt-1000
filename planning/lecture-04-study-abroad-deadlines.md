# Lecture 4 planning: study abroad deadlines

Notes for writing `lectures/lecture-04.qmd` (Sept. 18, 2026: degree plan; study abroad).

**Source:** application cycles for all programs accepting applications in [ATLAS](https://atlas.gatech.edu/_portal/program-discovery), Georgia Tech's education abroad portal. Pulled **September 16, 2026**. Deadlines can change; re-check a program's brochure page before putting a date on a slide.

## Key takeaways

- **The earliest undergraduate deadline is Oct. 12** ([Georgia Tech Europe, Spring 2027](https://atlas.gatech.edu/_portal/tds-program-brochure?programid=10215)). Sept. 18 is the only class meeting before it.
- **First-year students can apply to Georgia Tech Europe.** Per its brochure: minimum 2.5 GPA; first-year and transfer students without a GT GPA are accepted conditionally, confirmed or retracted after their first semester's GPA. Contact: [info@europe.gatech.edu](mailto:info@europe.gatech.edu).
- **Summer 2027 programs are the most realistic fit for first-years.** Their deadlines run Dec. 1 through Feb. 15.
- **Most exchange programs (Fall 2027 / academic year) close Feb. 1, 2027.**
- **Finding physics-friendly programs:** the [ATLAS program search](https://atlas.gatech.edu/_portal/program-discovery) has an "Academic Disciplines → Physics" filter. Not checked program by program here.
